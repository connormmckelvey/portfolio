// travel log

A map of places, trips,
and the stories tied to them.

This page is built to become your personal log of everywhere you have been. Click a pin or card to open the details, filter by year or search term, and replace the starter trips in trips.json with your own history.

starter workflow

  1. Add a trip entry in map/trips.json.
  2. Set coordinates, dates, notes, and photos.
  3. Refresh the page to see the new pin.
  4. Use the cards and filters to review trips.
