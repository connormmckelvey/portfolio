// currently building
active research

Vibrotactile Band
Teaching Large Motor Skills

A low-cost wearable armband system designed for the HERO Lab at Texas Robotics. Delivers direction-based haptic cues to guide motion through up to 4 vibration motor pods. Features a custom 3cm × 5cm Master Pod PCB with integrated charging, orientation tracking (IMU), and <1ms latency wireless communication. Built for under $25 per unit to enable accessible motor skill learning.
